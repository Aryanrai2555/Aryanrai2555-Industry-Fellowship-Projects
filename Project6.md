# Project 6 — Customer Analytics & Engagement Strategy

## Objective

**How engaged are customers, and are they coming back?**

## Brief Project Summary

Analyzed customer engagement across departments using customer behavior data, focusing on new, repeat, reactivated, and returning customers.

## Business Problem

- Limited visibility into department-level customer engagement.
- Need to identify departments with strong repeat and retention behavior.
- Need to understand customer return patterns and differences in shopping behavior across departments.

## Key Work Performed

- Analyzed new, repeat, and reactivated customer behavior.
- Calculated department-level customer counts and repeat rates.
- Studied next-month return rates to understand customer retention.
- Compared customer behavior across departments based on frequency, basket size, and return patterns.
- Identified different engagement patterns between high-frequency and more occasional departments.

## Important Insights / Findings

- Grocery & Staples had **10,973 active customers** and a **93.9% repeat rate**.
- Dairy & Frozen had **10,854 active customers** and a **93.4% repeat rate**.
- Beverages had **10,680 active customers** and a **93.4% repeat rate**.
- High-frequency departments showed stronger repeat and return behavior, while departments such as Fashion and Electronics showed more occasional shopping patterns.

## Business Impact / Interpretation

- Identifies departments with strong customer engagement.
- Supports customer retention and re-engagement strategies.
- Helps distinguish habitual shopping categories from more occasional purchase categories.
- Provides opportunities for cross-shopping and basket-building strategies.
