# Project 2 — Like-for-Like (LFL) Sales & Performance Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

A Power BI dashboard designed to compare **2026 vs 2025 sales performance** using identical-day matching.

The approach accounts for differences in weekdays between the two years, providing a more meaningful year-over-year performance comparison.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Traditional year-over-year comparisons using exact calendar dates can be misleading because the same date may fall on different weekdays across different years.

The project aimed to:

- Compare current-year and previous-year performance accurately.
- Reduce manual transaction-level analysis.
- Monitor sales, transactions, quantity, and ATV.
- Identify regional and store-format performance.
- Understand department and category performance.

---

## 🛠️ Key Work Performed

- Built an interactive Power BI dashboard.
- Implemented identical-day matching for 2026 vs 2025.
- Created KPI cards for:
  - Current Year Sales
  - Previous Year Sales
  - Sales Growth
  - Transactions
  - ATV
  - Quantity
- Created monthly trend analysis.
- Developed regional performance visualizations.
- Analyzed store-format contribution.
- Created geographic performance analysis.
- Built department and category summary tables.
- Added interactive slicers for analysis.

---

## 📊 Key Insights

- Current-year sales were approximately **$3M**, compared with approximately **$2M** in the previous year.
- Sales growth was approximately **19.3%**.
- Transactions increased by approximately **27.3%**.
- Quantity increased by approximately **33.4%**.
- ATV declined by approximately **6.3%**, from approximately **$356 to $334**.
- May 2026 showed approximately **23.1% YoY growth**.
- Supermarkets contributed approximately **71.39%**.
- Grocery & Staples was the leading category by current-year sales.

---

## 💡 Business Impact

The analysis helps management:

- Evaluate like-for-like business performance.
- Identify sales growth and transaction trends.
- Understand declining basket value.
- Identify store formats and categories contributing to growth.
- Support decisions around offers, combinations, and bundles to improve basket size.
