# Project 7 — Customer Analytics & RFM Segmentation

## Objective

**Which type of customer is this based on their purchasing behavior?**

## Brief Project Summary

Performed customer segmentation using RFM-based analysis to classify customers according to recency, frequency, monetary value, and related purchasing behavior.

## Business Problem

- Need to understand differences in customer value and engagement.
- Treating all customers in the same way can lead to inefficient targeting.
- Need to identify high-value customers, growth opportunities, and customers requiring re-engagement.

## Key Work Performed

- Analyzed Recency, Frequency, and Monetary metrics.
- Defined six customer segments:
  - VIP
  - Frequent Shoppers
  - Bulk Buyers
  - New
  - Occasional Buyers
  - Lapsers
- Created a customer segmentation Gold table with RFM and supporting behavioral metrics.
- Compared department penetration between VIP and Frequent Shoppers.
- Identified cross-selling and customer-growth opportunities.

## Important Insights / Findings

- VIP customers represented **6.7% of customers and 21.8% of revenue**.
- VIP + Frequent Shoppers represented **22.4% of customers and 52.8% of revenue**.
- Fashion & Lifestyle showed a **21.6 percentage-point penetration gap** between VIP and Frequent Shoppers.
- Electronics & Accessories showed a **20.7 percentage-point penetration gap**.
- Grocery & Staples, Beverages, and Dairy & Frozen showed 100% penetration for both VIP and Frequent Shoppers.

## Business Impact / Interpretation

- Enables targeted strategies for different customer segments.
- Helps identify customers who can be nurtured toward higher-value segments.
- Supports cross-selling and basket-growth opportunities.
- Provides a structured segmentation table for campaign targeting and future segment tracking.
