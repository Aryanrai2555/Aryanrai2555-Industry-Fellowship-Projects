# Project 1 — Retail Executive Summary Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

An interactive Power BI Executive Summary Dashboard developed using pre-processed data from Databricks SQL Gold tables.

The dashboard provides management with a centralized view of overall sales performance, loyalty customer contribution, regional store performance, payment habits, and monthly sales trends.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Management required a centralized and interactive reporting solution to:

- Reduce the time required for manual reporting.
- Understand the contribution of loyalty customers.
- Compare sales performance across regions and stores.
- Analyze customer payment preferences.
- Monitor sales trends over time.

---

## 🛠️ Key Work Performed

- Reviewed the Business Requirements Document (BRD) and wireframe.
- Worked with three pre-aggregated Gold-layer SQL tables.
- Built an interactive Power BI dashboard.
- Created DAX measures for:
  - Total Sales
  - Average Transaction Value (ATV)
  - Month-over-Month (MoM) Growth
- Developed KPI cards for executive-level monitoring.
- Analyzed loyalty vs. non-loyalty customer performance.
- Analyzed regional and store performance.
- Created geographic visualizations.
- Analyzed department performance.
- Analyzed payment methods.
- Created monthly sales trend visualizations.

---

## 📊 Key Insights

- Loyalty customers accounted for **58.43% of revenue**, representing a substantial contribution to overall sales.
- Loyalty customers generated an average transaction value of approximately **₹1.41K**.
- The analysis covered approximately **446K transactions**.
- The North region generated approximately **₹0.28 billion** in sales.
- Supermarkets contributed approximately **₹434.21M**, representing **69.14%** of sales.
- Household had the highest loyalty contribution at approximately **80.35%**.
- Grocery & Staples showed approximately **58.71% loyalty contribution** and generated around **₹0.30 billion** in sales.

---

## 💡 Business Impact

The dashboard provides management with:

- Centralized sales reporting.
- Better visibility into loyalty customer contribution.
- Regional and store-level performance insights.
- Improved understanding of payment behavior.
- Faster access to key business metrics for decision-making.
