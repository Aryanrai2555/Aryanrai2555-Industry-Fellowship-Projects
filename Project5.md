# Project 5 — Customer Analytics & Customer Lifetime Value

## Objective

**How valuable is a customer over their relationship with the business?**

## Brief Project Summary

Analyzed customer value and behavior with a focus on Customer Lifetime Value (CLV), loyalty, retention, acquisition, demographics, and customer lifecycle.

## Business Problem

- Limited understanding of customer value and lifetime contribution.
- Difficulty identifying high-value, loyal, at-risk, and churned customers.
- Need to understand customer behavior across loyalty, demographic, value, and lifecycle dimensions.

## Key Work Performed

- Analyzed Customer Lifetime Value (CLV), Average Transaction Value (ATV), customer value, and purchase frequency.
- Studied retention, churn, loyalty performance, and loyalty tiers.
- Analyzed customer acquisition quality, time to second purchase, and new customer contribution.
- Studied customer lifecycle stages, cohort retention, customer tenure, and reactivation.
- Considered customer behavior across demographics, regions, categories, and store formats.

## Important Insights / Findings

- Customer value depends on spending, purchase frequency, retention, and lifecycle stage.
- Loyalty and customer segments can show different spending and retention patterns.
- Lifecycle analysis helps distinguish new, growing, mature, at-risk, and churned customers.
- Acquisition quality can be evaluated by whether new customers return for subsequent purchases.

## Business Impact / Interpretation

- Helps identify high-value customer groups.
- Supports targeted retention and loyalty strategies.
- Provides a framework for improving customer acquisition and customer lifetime value.
- Helps marketing and CRM teams make more informed customer-focused decisions.
